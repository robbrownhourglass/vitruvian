About us

Our journey

A considered approach to conservation that respects craftsmanship, values longevity, and protects our built heritage for generations to come.

Who we are

We believe the best way to protect the past is to work with it, not against it.

Too often, modern materials seal, trap, and ultimately damage the very surfaces they’re meant to protect. We set out to offer an alternative.

Our materials are rooted in traditional methods, using natural, breathable lime that allows buildings to age as they were meant to. They work with the structure, not over it, preserving texture, character and integrity while offering lasting protection.

Sustainability sits at the core of what we do. By choosing materials that are low-impact, long-lasting and compatible with historic surfaces, we help reduce unnecessary repairs and protect what already exists.

This is more than a finish. It’s a considered approach to conservation, one that respects craftsmanship, values longevity, and supports the preservation of our cultural heritage for generations to come.

Meet the team

Craft and conservation, side by side

Vitruvian Lime brings together a master craftsman from a six-generation plastering family and a built heritage consultant dedicated to the holistic care of historic buildings.

Damien Condon

Founder & Master Craftsman

Carrying forward six generations of plastering and masonry craft, Damien Condon is the founder of Calx Renders Ltd. and a conservation specialist with more than 35 years’ experience in the conservation of historic buildings.

  • Professional Development Diploma in Historic Building Conservation and Repair, West Dean College
  • Professional Development Diploma in the Conservation and Repair of Architectural Structures and Building Conservation, West Dean College
  • C1 & C2 Conservation Masonry (SCQF, SQA)
  • C1 & C2 Working with Historic Materials, The Scottish Lime Centre

Caroline Kearns Gethings

Founder & Built Heritage Consultant

Caroline is a built heritage consultant specialising in conservation and sustainability, with over a decade of experience. She is the founder of Anú Heritage, a specialist consultancy dedicated to preserving Ireland’s built heritage through a proactive, holistic approach to conservation.

  • BA (Hons) in Culture and Heritage
  • Postgraduate Diploma in Applied Building Repair and Conservation, Trinity College Dublin
  • Professional Development Diploma in Historic Building Conservation and Repair, West Dean College
