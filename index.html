Our approach

We believe the best way to protect the past is to work with it, not against it.

Too often, modern materials seal, trap, and ultimately damage the very surfaces they’re meant to protect. Our materials are rooted in traditional methods, using natural, breathable lime that allows buildings to age as they were meant to.

It works with the structure, not over it, preserving texture, character and integrity while offering lasting protection.

Our materials

A complete lime system, from first coat to finish

Our lime putty products are soft, flexible and vapour-permeable, compatible with historic stone and brick and suited to new work where a non-cementitious finish is needed.

Mortars

Bedding, base bedding (hair or hemp), packing and pointing mortars, with fibre-reinforced options.

Plasters

Scud coat adhesion primers, scratch coats with animal hair or polypropylene fibres, and float coats.

Renders

Dub coats and float render coats for breathable external walls.

Lime putty

Traditional matured lime putty, a base for mortars, plasters and limewash.

Available in 20 kg buckets, quarter-ton and one-ton bags. Online ordering is coming soon; in the meantime, get in touch for prices and to place an order.

Why lime

Built to breathe

Breathable

Moisture moves through the wall and evaporates naturally, instead of being trapped inside solid masonry.

Flexible

Softer than cement, lime accommodates minor movement and protects the stone and brick around it.

Compatible

Made to work with historic materials, preserving the texture and character of traditional buildings.

Sustainable

Natural, low-impact and long-lasting, reducing unnecessary repairs and protecting what already exists.

ComplianceAll Vitruvian mortars, plasters, renders and washes are produced using a CE-certified lime binder (EN 459-1:2010). A full Statement of Compliance is available on request.
